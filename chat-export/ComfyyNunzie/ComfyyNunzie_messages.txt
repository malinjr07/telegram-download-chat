Here's a full analysis of your YAML and exactly what to tweak to hit ~6 hours on the RTX 6000.

## Issues Causing Slowness

1. `batch_size: 4` — This is your biggest problem

At 1024×768 resolution with WAN 14B in bf16, batch size 4 is almost certainly causing VRAM pressure and slowdowns (possibly even the OOM on H100 too). Drop this to batch_size: 2 or even 1 and compensate with more steps if needed. Counterintuitively, a lower batch size often trains *faster* wall-clock time on a single GPU. [github](https://github.com/tdrussell/diffusion-pipe/issues/152)

2. `gradient_checkpointing: false` — Turn this ON

With the 14B model at 1024×768, you need this. It trades a small compute overhead for massive VRAM savings, which will stabilize your run and prevent slowdowns from memory pressure: [mimicpc](https://www.mimicpc.com/learn/how-to-train-wan21-lora-with-diffusion-pipe)
gradient_checkpointing: true

3. Resolution `[1024, 768]` — Drop to `[512, 768]` or `[640, 512]`

This is a huge memory and compute multiplier. For a character LoRA on 106 still images, training at full 1024px gives minimal benefit. Dropping to 512–640px cuts VRAM and step time dramatically: [github](https://github.com/tdrussell/diffusion-pipe/issues/152)
resolution:
  - 768
  - 512

4. `low_vram: false` → Set to `true`

You're on an RTX 6000 (48GB), not an A100/H100. Enable this:
low_vram: true
This enables smarter memory management and offloading during the forward/backward pass. [stablediffusiontutorials](https://www.stablediffusiontutorials.com/2025/03/wan-lora-train.html)

5. `layer_offloading: false` → Enable it

This is your H100 OOM fix too. Enable layer offloading on the transformer:
layer_offloading: true
layer_offloading_transformer_percent: 0.5
Offloading 50% of transformer layers to RAM dramatically cuts peak VRAM usage. [reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/StableDiffusion/comments/1jbbn4j/how_much_memory_to_train_wan_lora/)

6. `steps: 2500` — This is actually fine

2,500 steps with 106 images and num_repeats: 1 is reasonable. Don't reduce this further or quality will suffer.

7. `save_every: 300` + `max_step_saves_to_keep: 60` — Reduce saves

Saving every 300 steps and keeping 60 saves means 8 saves total with constant disk I/O. Drop this to reduce overhead:
save_every: 500
max_step_saves_to_keep: 10

8. `sample_every: 400` — Sampling is expensive

With 6 sample prompts at 24 steps each generating 41 frames, every sample run takes several minutes. Reduce frequency:
sample_every: 800

## Your Optimised Config Changes (Summary)

| Parameter | Current | Change To |
|---|---|---|
| batch_size | 4 | 2 |
| gradient_checkpointing | false | true |
| resolution | [1024, 768] | [768, 512] |
| low_vram | false | true |
| layer_offloading | false | true |
| layer_offloading_transformer_percent | 1 | 0.5 |
| save_every | 300 | 500 |
| max_step_saves_to_keep | 60 | 10 |
| sample_every | 400 | 800 |

These combined should bring you well under 6 hours on the RTX 6000, and also fix the H100 OOM by preventing peak VRAM spikes from the batch size and layer loading. [reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/StableDiffusion/comments/1jbbn4j/how_much_memory_to_train_wan_lora/)

*What I've Learnt Generating High-Quality Videos*

Been spending a lot of time on high-quality video generation to increase the quality of content on Fanvue. While the SFW for my agency models is pretty spot on, the Fanvue NSFW content has been lacking for our rising star, which has been the center of my focus this past week.

*What I Learnt With Video Generation:*

1. The Foundation: The key to high-quality videos is the base images. It sounds like common sense now that I think about it, but it was a genuine part of video gen that I completely missed during my first couple of NSFW scripts.

2. Quality over Speed: Generating high quality at a slow speed is vastly superior to generating with lightning LoRAs. I have not found the best results with lightning LoRAs using WAN 2.2, which is what I’ve been using. Though they could be good for quickly testing generations, I just don’t find the trade-off for quality is worth it.

*Ideal Settings for 8 Seconds of High-Quality Video:*

* Steps: 25
* Split Step: 10
* Frames: 121
* Interpolation: x 4
* Overlap Frame: 1 (very important to decrease that visible transition from the first vid to the second)
* Frame Rate: 60

These settings give amazing results. One of the biggest tips would be focusing on close-up NSFW videos. Close-ups always look more real because the AI model is allowed to focus more on the anatomy and face. When extending videos with the face as a close-up, the model’s face will degrade less over time compared to extending videos where the face is far away.

3. The IPAdapter Rule: Always use an IPAdapter for your model’s face when generating extensions to your videos. You will need to generate an extremely high-quality portrait of your model’s face. I personally use Nano Banana to get this, just because the grain that Z-image often produces will conflict with the IPAdapter. If the IPAdapter sees grain on the model’s face, it will attach that grain to the model's identity. 💯
